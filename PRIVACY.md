# Privacy Policy — Fake Traveler

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

Fake Traveler does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal data. There is no
account, no analytics and no advertising.

## What stays on your device

The points you pick, your favorites, imported routes and settings are saved only on your phone and
are removed when you uninstall the app.

## Permissions

- **Location**: required by Android to run the simulation as a foreground service. The app does not
  read or record your real location.
- **Mock location** (selected by you in Developer options): used to provide the fake location to
  the system.
- **Notifications**: shows the ongoing simulation with a Stop button.
- **Internet**: downloads map tiles and searches addresses.

## Third-party services

Map tiles come from [OpenStreetMap](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/) and
[Esri](https://www.esri.com/en-us/privacy/overview); address search uses
[Photon by komoot](https://photon.komoot.io). These services receive the map area or the search
text you request, as any web request does, and are governed by their own privacy policies.

## Contact

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/DIEGOHORVATTI/FakeTraveler/issues>.

# Política de Privacidade — Fake Traveler

O Fake Traveler não coleta, não guarda em servidor, não vende e não compartilha dados pessoais. Não
há conta, análise de uso nem anúncios. Os pontos, favoritos, rotas e configurações ficam só no seu
celular. A permissão de localização é exigida pelo Android para manter a simulação em primeiro
plano; o app não lê nem registra a sua localização real. Os mapas vêm do OpenStreetMap e da Esri e
a busca de endereços usa o Photon (komoot), que recebem apenas a área do mapa ou o texto buscado.
